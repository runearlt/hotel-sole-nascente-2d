# Lake Garda visual redesign

A fictional eastern-shore neighbourhood inspired by Lake Garda; not a reproduction of a named hotel or surveyed location. Lake lies north of the playable district, bounded by a visible opposite shore and layered limestone ridges. Hotel remains northwest, bakery southwest and winery northeast, preserving all investigation targets and saved coordinates. A 6 m asphalt local road runs east–west, with raised stone pavements, hotel forecourt, south bakery spur and a gravel vineyard lane. The hotel rear terrace faces the lake. Vineyard rows rise on low terraces southeast of the winery.

Hotel: pale ochre mineral plaster, dark olive shutters, recessed glazing, limestone surrounds, tiled pitched roof, copper gutters and downpipes, shallow iron balconies, canvas entry canopy, rear café terrace. Reception: timber slat counter, framed lake-facing windows, warm lamps, luggage storage, business desk, travel photography and restrained upholstery. Open-front interiors use a consistent architectural cutaway; circulation and collisions remain explicit.

Bakery: sage striped fabric awning, recessed shopfront, glazed bread display, oven controls, stainless worktop, varied loaves/pastries, bags and crates. Winery: stone base, timber shutters, trellis/pergola, terraced vines and agricultural gate; barrel storage, bottle racks, service counter and tasting furniture inside.

Landscape: original continuous irregular mountain ridge geometry in three atmospheric layers, shoreline settlements, lake with animated normal-map ripples and environmental reflections, jetty/boat, branched olive trees and narrow cypresses, planted beds, climbing greenery. Late afternoon light with moderated exposure and warm shadows. Characters gain facial features, shirt details, segmented limbs and less rigid turning.

All new meshes, signs, foliage and supplementary textures are authored procedurally in this repository. Photographic plaster and paving base-color/OpenGL normal maps are self-hosted in surface-textures.js, resized to 512px WebP from the official Poly Haven 1K maps. Assets: Plastered Wall 04 (https://polyhaven.com/a/plastered_wall_04) and Cobblestone Floor 01 (https://polyhaven.com/a/cobblestone_floor_01), both CC0. No external asset requests are needed at runtime. Reference hotel photographs were consulted for design only; no hotel photograph or third-party model is embedded. Three.js remains MIT licensed; see public/vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt. Visual inspiration: official Malcesine tourism imagery, https://www.visitmalcesine.com/en/accommodation/venezia . GTA V is an interaction/detail reference only; no Rockstar assets are used.

Verification: actual rendering at desktop/mobile sizes, representative overview and close-up screenshots, collision/route tests, camera sightlines, workbook/conversation regressions, unchanged case/actor modules, and hosted commit/asset comparison. Phone settings reduce foliage density, pixel ratio and shadows. This is a detailed real-time architectural simulation, not a photogrammetric reconstruction or a GTA-scale production.
