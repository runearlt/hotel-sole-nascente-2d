Hotel Sole Nascente

The hotel is reviewing its room price for a night. You have been asked to gather the evidence and recommend a rate. The staff hold different parts of the information, so this introduction does not contain everything needed for your analysis.

Your investigation

Talk to Lucia, the hotel manager, about a reference price and the room requests recorded at that price. Marco, the reservations lead, has a separate comparison observation and can explain the conditions behind it. Elena, the operations manager, can discuss room capacity and the cost information available.

Type your own questions. Ask follow-up questions when you need clarification, and keep track of who provided each piece of evidence. Conversations in your current session are separate for each actor; a colleague does not automatically know what you discussed elsewhere. Your collected evidence appears in the notebook.

Your analysis

Prices are in EUR per room for one night. Quantities describe rooms requested for that night. Establish whether the observations can be compared, then assume a linear demand relationship and derive the demand function yourself. Check your function in the workspace before deriving total and marginal revenue and calculating point price elasticity at the reference price.

Choose a rate and predict room requests, actual room sales and room revenue. Explain how capacity, missing information and uncertainty affect your recommendation. Distinguish requested rooms from rooms that can actually be sold, and room revenue from profit. Save your reasoning and export the notebook for discussion.

Optional extensions

Marta and Paolo offer a separate bakery exercise about output and costs for a breakfast batch. Sofia and Giulia offer a wine purchasing exercise about prices, stock and a dinner forecast. Their figures are illustrative assumptions independent of your hotel room-rate decision. Complete the main hotel case first if you are following the core assignment.
